2. How the sim fills event-by-event replay · queue-position fills

Fills come from queue position, not from "price touched = filled".

Every resting order carries queue_pos — the shares sitting ahead of it at its own price.

3. The execution that works perfect-signal search · 100 days

Overview

The signal sets a target position; the execution layer moves the actual position there safely: linear target · one fixed lot · three resting-order rules (level / sticky / depth cap) · reduce-only outside the universe. The spread is one tick, so an order sits on the BBO or steps away; everything is sized in shares (a share settles at $1 or $0).

ParameterCurrentMeaning
lot_shares600shares per resting order
max_position3,000 (5 lots)cap per market
quote_offset_ticks0levels behind the touch; 0 = at the BBO
sticky_ticks2re-place only after drifting 2 levels
book_frac0.05each order ≤5% of visible depth
enter_thresholdp90-calibrated|signal| needed to open
clear_frac0.5unwind child order = half a lot

Evidence: the realized price move itself fed in as the signal (deliberate look-ahead — the ceiling decouples from signal quality), all 100 days.
