3. The execution that works perfect-signal search · 6 rounds · 100 days

The design

LayerWhat it does
signal → target positionthe target is a linear function of the signal, not a fixed size past a threshold
order sizeone fixed lot — a size that tracks the signal re-places on every event, and a re-place forfeits queue position
order managementsticky band · size capped by visible depth · level offset from the BBO
unwindingmarkets that leave the universe go reduce-only

Two venue facts behind it: the median spread is one tick, so an order sits on the BBO or steps away; and positions are sized in shares, not dollars — a share settles at $1 or $0, so N shares is $N of exposure at any price.

Fixing the settings under a perfect signal

Feed the realized price move itself in as the signal — deliberate look-ahead. The execution ceiling decouples from signal quality: if the signal were perfect, which settings are right — and which of those verdicts transfer back? Six rounds, 100 days.

lot = shares per order · book_frac = cap as share of visible depth · offset = levels behind the touch · sticky = re-place only after drifting this many levels

Size first. 1× → 12× lot: total ×7.9 and per-unit edge rises, 514 → 563 bp. What binds is the participation cap, not edge decay.

offset — keep it, but never tune it here. The perfect signal says drop it (total ×2.4 without it). A real signal needs it: the fills it gives up are precisely the expensive ones — hindsight has no expensive fills, so this verdict does not transfer.

sticky — transfers. Re-pricing on every BBO move: twice the orders, less money. Chasing costs the same whether the signal is good or not.

Current best: one fixed lot · one level behind the touch · sticky 2 · each order ≤5% of visible depth.

Open: three settings not yet searched; per-round participation not yet computed — the ceiling totals are not attainable numbers.
