3. The execution that works perfect-signal search · 6 rounds · 100 days

Overview

The signal sets a target position; the execution layer's job is to move the actual position there safely. The target is a linear function of the signal; the gap does not matter — only one fixed lot rests at a time; resting orders live under three rules (level offset · sticky re-pricing band · depth cap); markets that leave the universe go reduce-only. The spread is one tick, so an order sits on the BBO or steps away; everything is sized in shares — a share settles at $1 or $0, so N shares is $N of exposure at any price.

lot = shares per order · book_frac = cap as share of visible depth · offset = levels behind the touch · sticky = re-place only after drifting this many levels

① Signal → target position

Chosen: linear. |signal| past p90 opens, each further lot demands one more step of signal, capped at 5 lots — not a fixed size past a threshold.

Tried: freezing the target on a timer — no. Profit-taking already falls out of "target is a function of the current signal"; freezing switches that function off. The slope lean_per_lot has not been searched.

② Order size

Chosen: one fixed lot of 600 shares, never tracking the signal. A size that tracks the signal re-places on every event, and a re-place forfeits queue position.

Tried: lots at 1× / 6× / 12× — total ×7.9 while per-unit edge rises, 514 → 563 bp; adding size barely dilutes. What binds is the participation cap, not edge decay: 12× almost surely crosses the line, so 6×.

③ Where to rest (offset)

Chosen: one level behind the touch.

Tried: at the BBO — under the perfect signal, total ×2.4, so it looks right. But the fills the offset gives up are precisely the expensive ones (per-unit edge 578 vs 555), and hindsight has no expensive fills — this verdict does not transfer; with a real signal the offset stays. Also tried inverse-volatility pricing — no: the σ estimate is reliable where it does not matter and unreliable where it does (10× swings at thin prices).

④ Re-pricing restraint (sticky)

Chosen: re-place only after drifting 2 levels.

Tried: sticky 0 (re-place on every BBO move) — orders 1.57M → 3.23M for less money. Chasing costs the same whether the signal is good or not — this one transfers. Larger sticky keeps the queue but rests too far from the market.

⑤ Depth cap per order (book_frac)

Chosen: each order ≤5% of visible depth. The fill model has no authority over an order that would eat a large slice of the visible book.

Tried: 20% — total +8% under the perfect signal, but per-unit edge −11 bp, and larger child orders cross the participation line sooner.

⑥ Unwinding

Chosen: markets that leave the universe go reduce-only, worked off in half-lot child orders. clear_frac not yet settled.

Open: per-round participation not yet computed — the perfect-signal totals are not attainable numbers.
